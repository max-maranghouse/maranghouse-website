Nice To Meet You.

The reality for children suffering from chronic illness is challenging.

Many families simply cannot afford the permanent medical care, supervision, and reliable environment required for their children's well-being.

They need to be consuming these illnesses routes like an oral cavity, filled with daily monitors of visiting dialysis, and the excruciating waiting game for organ transplants.

In 1993, Marang House emerged as a beacon of hope, dedicated to bringing light into the lives of South Africa's seriously ill children.

Who We Are

About Us

A nurturing home where health and hope thrive side by side.

Our Mission

We provide a nurturing home environment for children between the ages of 4 and 14, empowering them to learn and manage their illnesses. They face serious health conditions that demand continuous monitoring and specialised treatments at a tertiary hospital.

Importantly, we ensure that their education remains a priority by taking them to the hospital daily, where they receive disease management training alongside traditional schooling.

What Makes Us Different

Marang House bridges the gap between medical care and everyday life. Children don't have to choose between their health and their education — we make both possible under one roof.

Since 1993, we have provided a beacon of hope for South Africa's most seriously ill children, ensuring they grow up with dignity, care, and opportunity.

Our Community

Lightkeepers

The Lightkeepers are the people who help us keep Marang House going — a community of supporters, donors, and advocates who believe every child deserves a home, health, and hope.

Be the Light

Every contribution — big or small — keeps the lights on at Marang House. Lightkeepers are the backbone of our community, ensuring children have a safe place to heal, learn, and grow.
