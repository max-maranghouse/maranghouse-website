# Content & Brand Audit — Marang House Website

**Date:** 2026-08-28

## VERDICT: FAIL (5 blockers)

## Blockers

1. **Unverified impact and donor-count claims are presented as settled facts.** The homepage publishes “300+ Children” and “1000+ Volunteers” (`app/page.tsx:437-443`) even though `AGENTS.md` explicitly says neither figure is independently verified. About repeats “hundreds of children” (`app/about/page.tsx:89`), while About and Lightkeepers say “A Thousand Lightkeepers” (`app/about/page.tsx:146`; `app/lightkeepers/page.tsx:195`), which reads as a current recurring-donor count without any source. A 2025 partner article says Marang House had welcomed “more than 100” children, underscoring that the 300+ figure needs primary confirmation rather than inference ([Hot Cares, 2025](https://hotcares.co.za/hot-cares-and-jet-ubunye-bring-hope-and-healing-to-marang-house/)). Remove the numeric claims until the organisation supplies dated records, or replace them with non-numeric copy such as “Serving children since 1998.” If “A Thousand Lightkeepers” is an aspiration, label it explicitly: “Help us build a circle of Lightkeepers.”

2. **New first-person quotations and a detailed biography are attributed to Salome without a documented interview/source.** About labels the section “In Her Own Words,” recounts a specific family death and career decision, and publishes several direct quotations (`app/about/page.tsx:109-150`); Lightkeepers republishes another attributed quote (`app/lightkeepers/page.tsx:167-172`). These words do not appear in the project’s source-of-truth documents, and the repo's testimonial warning demonstrates why attributed copy must not be invented or merged from conflicting drafts. Obtain Salome's written approval or an interview transcript and archive the provenance. Otherwise paraphrase only verified role/history and remove quotation marks/“In Her Own Words.”

3. **“SPONSOR A CHILD TODAY” does not lead to a child-sponsorship product.** The homepage CTA (`app/page.tsx:347-349`) links to `/lightkeepers`, which describes general recurring giving and then sends visitors to BackaBuddy. No named-child sponsorship, allocation model, safeguarding explanation, recurring amount, or sponsorship terms exist. This is materially misleading fundraising language. Replace it with “Become a Lightkeeper” or “Give Monthly” unless Marang House defines and approves a real child-sponsorship programme.

4. **The site promises a limited-edition keyring without confirmed eligibility, inventory, fulfilment, or platform permission.** “Be the first to get a limited-edition keyring” appears immediately beside the BackaBuddy CTA (`app/page.tsx:91-103`). BackaBuddy's current terms say its campaigns collect monetary donations and that offers of rewards for donations are not permitted ([BackaBuddy terms](https://backabuddy.co.za/home/links?fees_tab=first&tab=fees)). Remove the promise from the donation journey unless BackaBuddy confirms the arrangement and Marang House documents who qualifies, quantity, delivery geography/cost, privacy handling, and stock limits. A safe replacement is: “Join the Circle of Light as a monthly donor.”

5. **The Section 18A tax-deductibility claim lacks the approval evidence needed to publish it safely.** Donate says donations are tax-deductible and that a certificate can be issued (`app/donate/page.tsx:109-114`); the same assertion is currently public on the separate emergency landing page. The repo records a PBO number but no Section 18A approval letter/reference or receipt process. SARS states that PBO status alone is not enough: eligibility depends on separate Section 18A approval and a compliant receipt ([SARS PBO guidance](https://www.sars.gov.za/businesses-and-employers/tax-exempt-institutions/public-benefit-organisations/), [SARS application guidance](https://www.sars.gov.za/businesses-and-employers/tax-exempt-institutions/application-for-section-18a/)). Confirm the current approval letter and operational ability to issue compliant receipts before launch. Until then, remove the claim; after confirmation, use precise conditional wording rather than implying every payment automatically qualifies.

## Major

1. **Absolute allocation and service promises overstate what a donation can guarantee.** Examples include “every contribution goes directly to the children” (`app/donate/page.tsx:24-27`), “Every rand goes directly to the children” (`app/page.tsx:404-407`), “Every rand goes directly to keeping Marang House running” (`app/lightkeepers/page.tsx:83-87`), “no appointment is ever missed” (`app/lightkeepers/page.tsx:91-95`), and “never have to tell a child there’s no room” (`app/lightkeepers/page.tsx:160-164`). They ignore payment-processing/operating realities and promise outcomes outside a donor's control. Replace with evidence-safe wording: “Your gift helps fund children's care and the day-to-day running of Marang House”; “helps keep hospital transport available”; and “helps Marang House plan capacity and care with greater confidence.”

2. **The B-BBEE benefit is stated too broadly.** Donate says corporate donors “can earn B-BBEE scorecard points” and elsewhere promises “B-BBEE recognition” (`app/donate/page.tsx:116-120, 142-147`). Recognition depends on the measured entity's applicable code, the nature of the contribution, beneficiary evidence, and verification. The B-BBEE Commission's SED guidance says full-value recognition depends on qualifying beneficiary impact and other criteria ([B-BBEE Commission educational material](https://www.bbbeecommission.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Educational-Material-9-Socio-Economic-Development.pdf)). Suggested replacement: “Depending on your organisation's applicable scorecard and verification requirements, qualifying contributions may be recognised under socio-economic development. Contact us for current supporting documentation.”

3. **The named supporter showcase needs a recency/permission check before relaunch.** The homepage describes eight people/organisations as people who “help us transform lives” (`app/page.tsx:256-334`). The names are inherited from the legacy static site, so they are not fabricated by this branch, but the audit found no dates, relationship descriptions, or evidence that every party remains a current supporter and consents to present-day endorsement. Confirm each entry with Marang House, then label the section “Past and present supporters” if the relationships are historical.

## Minor

1. **Five supporter portraits are visible initial-based substitutes.** The “MW,” “JJC,” “NW,” “RD,” and “PCI” circles (`app/page.tsx:279-333`) look like unfinished placeholder avatars beside real photos. Either obtain approved logos/portraits or use one deliberate, consistent supporter-card treatment for every entry.

2. **A business-critical address is duplicated rather than read from the source of truth.** Contact hardcodes the address in page metadata, map URL/title, and visible body (`app/contact/page.tsx:11, 130-152`) instead of using `ORGANISATION.address`. It matches today, but violates the repo rule intended to prevent drift. The same principle applies to adding the opening hours to `site-data` if those hours are meant to be canonical.

3. **Tagline and localisation drift slightly.** The shared source says “Fostering Health, Providing Hope.” while the header hardcodes “Fostering health. Providing hope.” (`components/SiteHeader.tsx:48`). Homepage uses American “volunteer program” (`app/page.tsx:383`) while the repo otherwise uses South African/British forms such as “programme.” Reuse the canonical tagline and standardise on “programme.”

4. **One Lightkeeper sentence is grammatically tangled.** “Monthly donors who keep the light of home shining, joy and love…” (`app/page.tsx:93-97`) obscures the intended idea. Suggested replacement: “The Marang Circle of Light is a community of monthly donors who help keep home, joy and love within reach for children receiving treatment away from their families.”

5. **Social profiles remain an unresolved content decision.** No Facebook or Instagram URL appears, consistent with `AGENTS.md`. This is not a broken-link finding because the site does not render fake icons; confirm whether the organisation wants verified social profiles added before handover.

6. **Brand colour implementation mixes tokens with many literal values.** The canonical blue, orange, yellow, red, purple, and cream values are correctly declared, but repeated gradient stops, near-whites, dark blues, and inline SVG/contact colours bypass the token layer (`app/globals.css`; `app/contact/page.tsx:87,124-125`). The Canva match may make some literals intentional; document them as secondary tokens so future edits do not create accidental near-misses.

## What passed

- **No obvious filler content:** Deployable markup contains no Lorem Ipsum, TODO/FIXME/TBD, example domain, test contact details, or the unverified Press headlines. `/press` remains a 404 stub and no testimonial section is rendered. Dead legacy Press/Testimonial CSS exists but exposes no public copy.
- **Core organisation facts:** Name, phone, email, registration identifiers, EFT details, and BackaBuddy destination agree across rendered pages and the `lib/site-data.ts` source of truth. Footer and Contact use the shared values for phone/email/registration facts. No Rad Lemon contact detail leaked into the client site.
- **Resolved age range:** All current user-visible references use the confirmed 7–14 range; the outdated archive value is not rendered.
- **Founding/capacity baseline:** The 1998 founding year and 12-child capacity are supported by multiple historical/current public references; 28 years is consistent with the current date. This does not validate the separate 300+/1000+ claims.
- **Donation platform:** GivenGain is absent. All donation CTAs that purport to use BackaBuddy point to the documented Marang Circle of Light campaign, which returned 200 during the health audit.
- **Links:** Internal, donation, WhatsApp, email, phone, map, and sampled Cloudinary links resolved or were syntactically correct. No fake social-platform homepage link is rendered.
- **Brand foundation:** The declared primary brand blue is exact, the additional documented orange/yellow/red/purple/cream tokens are present, and the intended Fredoka, Nunito, and Permanent Marker families are configured through `next/font`. Visual review showed a coherent Canva-derived system across all five mobile pages. No banned visual pattern is documented in the project.
- **Cloudinary delivery:** The deployable asset URLs use the shared `f_auto,q_auto` transform or the documented `f_png,q_auto` SVG-rasterisation exception. Performance-specific image sizing findings are recorded once in the site-health report rather than duplicated here.
- **Voice:** Most copy is warm, direct, child-centred, and specific about housing, treatment, education, meals, and transport. It avoids agency jargon such as “best-in-class,” “synergy,” and “cutting-edge.”

## Coverage gap

- The audit had no access to Marang House board/staff approvals, annual reports, audited beneficiary/volunteer totals, supporter consent records, the Section 18A approval letter/receipt workflow, B-BBEE verification documentation, keyring stock/fulfilment plan, or the BackaBuddy campaign dashboard. Those primary records—not archived mockups or web search—must close the blockers.
- Search results could confirm Salome's long-standing role and locate older, different public quotations, but not authenticate the new quotations used in this branch. Lack of a search hit is not proof that an internal interview did not occur; retain the copy only if the transcript/approval exists.
- Public sources contain older and conflicting age/capacity/beneficiary figures. Per project instruction, the user's confirmed 7–14 age range remains authoritative; external references were used only to identify where fresh primary verification is needed.
- Visual review used rendered mobile screenshots and source inspection, not the original Canva design file. Exact pixel/asset conformance was outside this content audit; brand-token conformance was checked against `AGENTS.md`/`CLAUDE.md`.
